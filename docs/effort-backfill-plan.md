## The plan — two arms, one budget

**Ratchet arm**: one cell per usability-gate defect model, at its busiest category and the `default` pointer, cheapest lane first. Saying so is the point. Each day reserves 25% of its budget for it, so the defect count falls from day 1 instead of only at the end of the effort arm.
