5. WHY IMPLEMENTING THE ROW AS WRITTEN WOULD REGRESS THE STORE
   data/tables/model_perf.jsonl is a derived artifact. Loading it as a "first-class base
   table, then supplementing with the UNPIVOT rows" makes the seed ingest its own previous
   output, which re-admits rows the current seed deliberately drops:
     - the all-zero placeholder purge (scripts/router_seed.py:561-577 — ten neuralwatt lanes
       and gpt-oss-20b were floored at tier -5 by a default-0.0 import);
     - family/twin dedupe and carried-note collapsing (:250-299);
     - the retired/archived/disabled lane filter inside the UNPIVOT (:551-552).
   Net effect: performance evidence could only ratchet upward and stale lane evidence would
   resurrect — a correctness loss, not a parity win.
