6 · Why this is the mechanism

An envelope only measures the items we thought to list. This takes the surfaces as input and derives structure, which is why it found the release gap, the unused evidence interface, the limbo branches and a red build at origin — none of which anyone filed. Two properties make it a scale rather than another status report:

  • The film is re-takeable and comparable. A film is a snapshot with a timestamp; the interesting signal is what persists across films. A fracture still present in the third film is systemic; the same reading in one film may be a bad day.
  • The observer is not the author, and the surface is not ours. Tags, releases, CI conclusions, HTTP codes and ledger aggregates are read, never asserted. No row can author an observation.

The rule that keeps it honest is the one already filed as TR-252: no item may be closed by reference. A finding is closed by an observation in the next film, not by a row saying it was fixed.
