But the machine was paid for activity, not outcome: 80,190 ticks, 56% failing or timing out, and only 3.6% leaving a code commit. Not one of 350 rows carries an evidence run, and 146 closed with zero acceptance criteria - so "done" cost nothing to assert and nothing to check. That is the mechanism behind the miss: the fleet was graded on whether a tick ran, not on whether the thing you asked for started working.

Each round produced a report that read like progress and a behaviour that did not change.
