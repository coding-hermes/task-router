4 · Coverage, honestly

measured over the last 3,000 ledger lines (24 proxied rows in the tail):

chain_length set    : 38%

skipped_hops_detail : 38%

no-hops outcome     : 33%

Two honest readings, neither of them flattering:

  • 33% no-hops is dominated by the missing-registry window — rows written while the service had no registry at all. Those are history, not current behaviour: every row since the deploy is served.
  • 0% cost coverage is real and not a bug in the proxy: the lanes being served are :free models with no published price, so the honest answer is null plus a reason. Until the 22 priceable free lanes are priced (TR-179), the cost-per-task-by-complexity-band view has no numerators on those hops.

Also visible in the ledger while measuring: 1 proxied row among 400 sampled earlier — i.e. almost no production traffic flows through the proxy, because the scheduler is still pointed elsewhere. That is the re-flip, and it is deliberate.

5 · What is still not proven

the flip gate — unchanged
  1. The density rehearsal has never been run. My largest run remains 3 requests at concurrency 1; the 09-25 lockup happened at 6.1× call density (~5.8/min). Everything else in this report is a design argument until a burst at that density holds or breaks.
  2. TR-194 — the degraded-path proof for unservable ratings (69 of 95 rated rows today). The incident's proximate trigger.
  3. The registry-absence guard — new today from this measurement: the service gate can be red for hours silently. Nothing watches gate.valid.
  4. The re-flip itself — pointing the scheduler at 9391. Owner's call; never auto-flipped.

7 · Verdict

The proxy features are real, deployed, and verified. The flood guard is enforcing at its cap, session keys persist, session identities can't collide, the chain and its exclusion detail reach the rows, unpriced cost is null-with-a-reason, and the whole live path passes 11/11 with 1.00 amplification at a fraction of this morning's latency.

The gap that mattered today was not features — it was deployment. 128 commits of proxy work, including three fixes that came directly from the incidents, were sitting unployed behind a service with no registry, quietly returning no-hops. "Written" and "running" are different states, and only one of them is visible from the board.

What to bet on next: the density rehearsal, because it is the only remaining question with a chance of changing the answer. The re-flip is safe to attempt the moment that number exists — and unsafe to attempt before it.
